    Trust and accuracy

    Transparent citation help, not blind automation

    CiteJury explains citation components and keeps privacy central. Users should verify important citations against official court, university, journal, publisher, or style-guide requirements.

    Clear limitations

    Citations are generated within labelled scopes. Supreme Court neutral citation token formatting is stricter; reporter-style citations still require source verification before formal use.

    Careful updates

    Citation behaviour is documented and checked carefully so formatting changes can be handled consistently over time.

    Privacy-first

    Citation drafts are processed on your device, with clear labels for rule scope, limits, and verification needs.
