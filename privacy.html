Privacy

Privacy Policy

CiteJury is designed to keep citation drafting simple and private. The citation generator currently runs in your browser and does not require account creation or citation form submission to CiteJury.

Information entered into the citation form

Citation form inputs are processed in the browser. CiteJury does not provide user accounts and does not intentionally store citation drafts.

Advertising and analytics

Ad spaces are currently reserved but inactive. CiteJury does not currently load third-party advertising or analytics scripts, and inactive ad slots stay hidden from users unless explicitly activated later.

Future advertising readiness

CiteJury is prepared for a future Google AdSense review. If ads are approved and enabled later, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit, and measure ads. Any activation must use a real publisher ID, a matching ads.txt file, privacy review, and a live production smoke test.

Users should review Google's advertising information to understand how Google uses advertising cookies and ad personalization controls.

Security headers

CiteJury uses production security headers for supported static hosts, including a conservative Content Security Policy that blocks unapproved third-party scripts by default.

Important note

Browsers and hosting platforms may create normal technical logs outside CiteJury's citation generator. CiteJury does not use those logs to build user citation profiles.

Advertising status

Advertisements are prepared but not active unless explicitly enabled after owner approval, AdSense approval, privacy review, and security-header review.
