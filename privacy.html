Privacy

Privacy Policy

CiteJury is designed as a browser-first static website. The citation generator currently runs in your browser and does not require account creation, backend form submission, or a database.

Information entered into the citation form

The current static version processes citation form inputs in the browser. No custom CiteJury backend stores citation form data, and the site does not provide user accounts.

Advertising and analytics

Ad spaces are currently reserved but inactive. CiteJury does not currently load third-party ad network scripts or analytics scripts. Third-party advertising or analytics tools must not be enabled without a future approved EP/ADR, privacy review, and production configuration update.

Future advertising readiness

If advertising is approved in the future, the privacy policy must be updated before activation to identify the relevant provider, disclose data collection and sharing, and explain cookies or similar technologies where applicable.

Security headers

CiteJury uses production security headers for supported static hosts, including a conservative Content Security Policy that blocks unapproved third-party scripts by default.

Important note

If the hosting provider or browser collects technical logs, those are controlled by the relevant platform, not by a custom CiteJury backend.
