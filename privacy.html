Privacy Policy

CiteJury is designed as a browser-first static website. The citation generator currently runs in the user's browser and does not require account creation or backend form submission.

Information entered into the citation form

The current static version processes citation form inputs in the browser. No backend server is used by CiteJury to store citation form data.

Advertising and analytics

Ad spaces are currently reserved but inactive. Third-party advertising or analytics tools should not be enabled without a future approved EP/ADR.

Important note

If the hosting provider or browser collects technical logs, those are controlled by the relevant platform, not by a custom CiteJury backend.
