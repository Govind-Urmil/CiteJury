# Citation Accuracy References

CiteJury should prefer transparent citation logic and avoid pretending to be an official legal authority.

## Practical verification sources
Users should verify citations against:
- The original judgment or statute text
- The official court website where applicable
- Reporter citation details
- University style guides
- Journal or publisher guidelines
- Moot court rules
- Required citation manuals such as OSCOLA, Bluebook, or local legal citation requirements

## Current project rule
CiteJury may format and explain citations, but it must not claim official authority or guaranteed legal correctness.
