# Ad Monetization Readiness

CiteJury is intended to remain free for users. Future monetization may use responsible, non-intrusive advertising.

## Current Status

Ads are **not active**.

EP-013 only reserves layout-safe ad slots so future ad integration does not disrupt the citation workflow.

## Locked Rules

- No third-party ad scripts without explicit approval.
- No backend introduced for ads.
- No ads inside active form fields or citation output controls.
- No popups, forced interstitials, or deceptive placements.
- Ads must not reduce citation usability or accessibility.
- Ads must not compromise the privacy-first promise.

## Reserved Slots

- `ad-homepage-learning`
- `ad-guide-footer`

## Future Approval Required

Before enabling real ads, create a future ADR covering:
- Chosen ad network
- Privacy impact
- Performance impact
- Legal/compliance review
- User experience boundaries
