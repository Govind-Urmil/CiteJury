# Search and Ads Next Steps After EP-049

## Search indexing

Proceed manually after EP-049 is live:

1. Add `https://citejury.work-on.workers.dev` as a URL-prefix property in Google Search Console.
2. Use a Google-provided verification method.
3. Submit `https://citejury.work-on.workers.dev/sitemap.xml`.
4. Inspect the homepage URL.
5. Repeat in Bing Webmaster Tools if desired.

Do not add fake verification tokens to the repository. Use only real tokens from the chosen webmaster account.

## Ads

EP-048/EP-049 only prepare the framework. They do not guarantee approval or revenue.

Before activation:

- Let the site be indexed.
- Confirm content quality and policy readiness.
- Apply for AdSense or another approved network.
- Add only real publisher and slot IDs.
- Publish a real `ads.txt` generated from the provider's account details.
- Run a live smoke test.

## Passive revenue expectation

Passive revenue depends on traffic, approval, content quality, policy compliance, geography, ad demand, and user behavior. The site is technically prepared for low-maintenance monetization, but revenue is not guaranteed.
