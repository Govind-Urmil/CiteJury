# Citation Accuracy Framework

## Purpose
CiteJury must earn user trust through transparent, conservative citation assistance.

## Core Rules
1. Never claim official authority.
2. Never claim guaranteed legal correctness.
3. Never invent missing citation components.
4. Explain which components were used.
5. Require verification for formal use.
6. Prefer original judgments, legislation, official court material, and the citation manual required by the user's institution or publisher.

## Authority Hierarchy
For verification, prefer:
1. Original legal source or official publication.
2. Official court or government source.
3. Required institutional/publisher citation guide.
4. Recognised citation manual.
5. Reputable secondary explanation.

## Product Position
CiteJury is a citation formatting and learning assistant, not a substitute for authoritative legal research or mandatory citation rules.

## Engineering Rule
A citation format should not be expanded merely because it looks plausible. New rules require documented rationale and test coverage.
