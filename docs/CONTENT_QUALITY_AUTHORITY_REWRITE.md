# EP-037 Content Quality and Authority Rewrite

EP-037 rewrites public guide content from generic/template pages into practical, citation-authority-oriented guidance.

## Scope

- Rewrote 15 public guide pages with source-first citation habits, field checklists, examples, and limitations.
- Added explicit trust-first language so users understand that generated citations are drafts requiring verification for important work.
- Clarified that CiteJury supports scoped formats only and does not claim full Bluebook, AGLC, or McGill generation.
- Preserved inactive ad placeholders without activating any ad network or external script.
- Added `assets/data/content-quality.json` for machine-readable release tracking.

## Authority basis

Content is aligned with the EP-032 to EP-036 authority foundation: documented SCI neutral citation scope, scoped OSCOLA support, and cautious treatment of reporter and international citation systems.

## Performance and maintenance

No backend, database, build step, runtime dependency, analytics, active ads, or external scripts were added. The pages remain static HTML/CSS and are suitable for unattended free/static hosting.

## Limitations

EP-037 improves public educational content. It does not expand generator support beyond the citation rules already scoped in the engine and test corpus.
