Trust and Accuracy

Built for clarity, privacy, and verification.

CiteJury is designed to help users format and understand legal citations. It does not replace official court, university, journal, publisher, or style-guide requirements.

Accuracy approach

CiteJury uses transparent browser-side citation rules and explains which citation parts are used. Important citations should always be verified against the original source and required citation style.

Privacy approach

The citation generator runs in the browser. The current static architecture does not require users to create accounts or submit citation form data to a backend server.

Bug reports

If a user reports a bug, Govind will review it and bring it back into the engineering workflow so it can be fixed in a future EP. The project aims to prevent bugs upfront through defensive code, QA checklists, and simple static architecture.

Legal disclaimer

CiteJury is an educational citation-formatting tool. It is not legal advice and should not be treated as a final authority for filing, publishing, or academic submission.
