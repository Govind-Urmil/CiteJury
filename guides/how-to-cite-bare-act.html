How to Cite a Bare Act or Statute

Learn how to cite Indian statutes, bare acts, sections, and provisions clearly.

Basic structure

A statute citation usually identifies the Act name, year, and relevant section or provision.

Example

Indian Evidence Act, 1872, s. 3.

Practical note

Use the exact Act title and year. For precise legal writing, include section, sub-section, clause, or explanation where relevant.

Educational use only.

This guide explains citation structure. Always verify final citations against the relevant court, university, journal, or publisher requirement.
