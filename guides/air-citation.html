CiteJury Authority Guide

AIR Citation Guide

AIR citations identify cases using the AIR reporter family. Because reporter series and court identifiers can be easy to confuse, CiteJury treats AIR output as a structured draft that needs source verification.

Trust-first note: CiteJury is a drafting and learning tool. Important citations should be verified against the original authority and the required style guide before submission.

Common AIR components

Reporter abbreviation, year, court abbreviation, and page number.

Case name and pinpoint page/paragraph where required.

Court clarity for Supreme Court and High Court materials.

CiteJury scope

CiteJury can format common AIR-style components supplied by the user.

It does not independently verify reporter pagination, court abbreviations, or whether a decision appears in AIR.

Verification

Check the reporter source, official judgment, or required institutional guide.

Do not mix AIR court abbreviations with unrelated reporter formats.
