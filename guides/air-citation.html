AIR citation anatomy

AIR Year Court Page

Example anatomy: AIR 2023 SC 154

  • AIR: All India Reporter.
  • 2023: AIR reporter year.
  • SC: court abbreviation, commonly Supreme Court.
  • 154: opening page in AIR.

Why court abbreviation matters

The court abbreviation is not decorative. It tells the reader which court's report is being cited. Do not invent or expand abbreviations without checking the source.

Required information in CiteJury

  • Case title: official case name.
  • AIR year: the reporter year.
  • Court abbreviation: for example SC where appropriate.
  • AIR page number: opening page in the reporter.

Common mistakes

  • AIR SC 154 — missing year.
  • AIR 2023 154 — missing court abbreviation.
  • 2023 AIR SC — missing page number.
  • Using the judgment date as the AIR year without verification.

AIR and parallel citations

A case may appear in AIR, SCC, SCR, or have a neutral citation. These are not interchangeable strings. They represent different citation paths. CiteJury helps format the selected path, but users must decide which path their institution requires.
