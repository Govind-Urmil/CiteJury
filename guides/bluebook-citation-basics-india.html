CiteJury Authority Guide

Bluebook Citation Basics for Indian Law Students

The Bluebook is an important legal citation system, especially in US legal writing. CiteJury currently treats Bluebook as an authority family for future expansion rather than a fully supported public generator style.

Trust-first note: CiteJury is a drafting and learning tool. Important citations should be verified against the original authority and the required style guide before submission.

What to understand first

Bluebook rules vary by source type and jurisdiction.

A correct Bluebook citation may require tables, abbreviations, court identifiers, and source-specific rules.

For Indian materials, your university or journal may adapt Bluebook rather than apply it directly.

CiteJury scope

CiteJury does not currently claim full Bluebook generation.

Where Bluebook is mentioned, it is for education, authority tracking, and future scoped implementation.

Safe use

Use CiteJury for structured drafting and learning.

Check final Bluebook citations against the official Bluebook and your institution’s instructions.

Examples and checks

Source-first habit

Identify source type before choosing any Bluebook rule.

Verification note

Do not submit a Bluebook citation without checking the required rule/table.

Educational use only.

This guide explains citation structure and verification habits. It is not legal advice and does not replace a court, journal, university, publisher, or competition citation rule.
