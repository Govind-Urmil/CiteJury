CiteJury Authority Guide

Moot Court Memorial Citation Guide

Moot memorial citations must be consistent, verifiable, and aligned with the competition rules. A polished format cannot rescue an inaccurate authority.

Trust-first note: CiteJury is a drafting and learning tool. Important citations should be verified against the original authority and the required style guide before submission.

Before formatting

Read the moot rulebook for required citation style, font, footnote, and bibliography rules.

Create a table of authorities early.

Use primary sources for important propositions.

Record pinpoints for every quoted or heavily relied-on proposition.

Authority hierarchy

Prefer binding cases and statutes where relevant.

Use persuasive foreign or academic sources only where appropriate to the proposition.

Check whether the moot problem restricts sources or citation style.

Final citation audit

Check every footnote for party names, year, reporter/neutral citation, court, and pinpoint.

Confirm every source in the table of authorities appears in the memorial and vice versa.

Remove duplicate or inconsistent short forms.

Examples and checks

Memorial checklist

Rulebook + source table + pinpoints + final footnote audit.

CiteJury role

Generate structured drafts; manually verify competition-specific formatting.
