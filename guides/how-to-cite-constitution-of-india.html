How to Cite the Constitution of India

Understand how to cite articles and provisions from the Constitution of India in legal writing.

Basic structure

A constitutional citation should identify the Constitution and the specific article, clause, schedule, or provision being referenced.

Example

Constitution of India, art. 21.

Tip

When writing for a university, journal, or court, follow the required style guide for abbreviations and pinpoint references.

Educational use only.

This guide explains citation structure. Always verify final citations against the relevant court, university, journal, or publisher requirement.
