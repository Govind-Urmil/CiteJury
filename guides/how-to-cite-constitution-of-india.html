How to Cite the Constitution of India

Constitutional citations should point the reader to the exact article, clause, sub-clause, schedule, or amendment being discussed.

Trust-first note: CiteJury is a drafting and learning tool. Important citations should be verified against the original authority and the required style guide before submission.

Basic structure

Identify the Constitution of India.

Add the article number, clause, sub-clause, schedule, or part where relevant.

Use the abbreviation required by your court, university, journal, or competition.

Common pinpoints

Article references, such as art. 21.

Clause references, such as art. 19(1)(a).

Schedule references, such as sch. VII, List I, Entry 82.

Verification

Confirm the current text of the provision where amendments may matter.

For constitutional cases, cite both the constitutional provision and the judgment supporting your proposition.

Examples and checks

Article citation

Constitution of India, art. 21.

Clause citation

Constitution of India, art. 19(1)(a).

Educational use only.

This guide explains citation structure and verification habits. It is not legal advice and does not replace a court, journal, university, publisher, or competition citation rule.
