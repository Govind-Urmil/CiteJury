SCC Citation Guide

Understand the common structure of Supreme Court Cases citations and how CiteJury helps format citation components.

Understanding the citation

Legal citations help readers identify and locate authorities accurately. The exact structure depends on the source, reporter, jurisdiction, and citation standard being used.

Core components

A citation may include the case or source name, decision or publication year, volume, reporter abbreviation, starting page, and a pinpoint reference.

Use CiteJury

CiteJury provides a browser-first workspace for generating and understanding citations. Important citations should always be checked against the applicable court, university, journal, or publisher requirements.
