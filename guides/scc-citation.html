What an SCC citation usually contains

Example anatomy: (2023) 7 SCC 154

  • 2023: report year.
  • 7: SCC volume.
  • SCC: reporter abbreviation.
  • 154: opening page of the reported case.

When SCC is useful

SCC citations are useful when your assignment, memorial, article, or court document expects a law-report citation and you have verified the case details from the reporter or a reliable legal database. SCC is different from a neutral citation: a neutral citation identifies the judgment independently of any reporter.

Required information in CiteJury

  • Case title: the official reported case name.
  • SCC report year: the year used in the citation.
  • SCC volume: the volume number.
  • Opening page: the first page of the report.

Common mistakes

  • 2023 SCC 154 — missing volume.
  • (2023) SCC 154 — still missing volume.
  • (2023) 7 SCC — missing opening page.
  • Using a paragraph number or PDF page as the SCC opening page.

SCC versus AIR versus neutral citation

SCC and AIR are reporter citations. A Supreme Court neutral citation such as 2023 INSC 154 identifies the decision without relying on a reporter. If you have both, use the format required by your institution and consider mentioning the parallel citation only where appropriate.
