CiteJury Authority Guide

SCC Citation Guide

SCC citations are widely used in Indian legal writing. CiteJury can help structure common SCC-style components, but the final citation should be checked against the reporter or required style guide.

Trust-first note: CiteJury is a drafting and learning tool. Important citations should be verified against the original authority and the required style guide before submission.

Common SCC components

Year, volume, reporter abbreviation, and starting page.

Case name and court context where required by the writing format.

Pinpoint page or paragraph when you rely on a specific passage.

CiteJury scope

CiteJury formats SCC-style component drafts only when the required fields are supplied.

It does not claim to verify reporter pagination, parallel citations, editorial notes, or whether a case is reported in SCC.

Best practice

Verify the citation against the actual SCC report, original judgment, or authorised database.

Use the pinpoint used by your source, especially in submissions and memorials.

Examples and checks

SCC-style component order

(Year) Volume SCC Page

Manual verification note

Reporter availability and page number must be checked against the source.

Educational use only.

This guide explains citation structure and verification habits. It is not legal advice and does not replace a court, journal, university, publisher, or competition citation rule.
