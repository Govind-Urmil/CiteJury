Legal Citation Guide for Law Students

A simple legal citation guide for Indian law students learning case, statute, article, and web-source citation.

Why citations matter

Legal citations let readers find the authority you rely on and assess the strength of your legal argument.

Common sources

Students commonly cite judgments, statutes, constitutional provisions, books, journal articles, reports, and websites.

Best habit

Generate a draft citation, then verify it against the style guide required by your teacher, university, journal, or moot court.

Educational use only.

This guide explains citation structure. Always verify final citations against the relevant court, university, journal, or publisher requirement.
