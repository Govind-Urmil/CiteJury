CiteJury Authority Guide

OSCOLA Citation Guide for Indian Law Students

OSCOLA is an Oxford legal citation guide primarily designed around UK legal materials, with general principles for other sources. CiteJury supports only scoped OSCOLA cases, legislation, books, and journal-style examples where fields are clear.

Trust-first note: CiteJury is a drafting and learning tool. Important citations should be verified against the original authority and the required style guide before submission.

What OSCOLA expects

Minimal punctuation and footnote-oriented citation.

Cases commonly begin with party names and then citation details.

Legislation, books, journals, and online sources each have their own rules and examples.

CiteJury scope

CiteJury supports limited OSCOLA-style output for clearly identified source types.

Bluebook, AGLC, and McGill are registered as authority families, but CiteJury does not yet claim public generator support for them.

Indian student workflow

Use OSCOLA only if your university, journal, or competition asks for it.

For Indian materials, verify whether your institution wants adapted OSCOLA, Indian law-report conventions, or a custom rulebook.

Examples and checks

OSCOLA case habit

Party names first, then citation details and pinpoint if required.

Manual verification note

Check the official OSCOLA guide and your institution’s local instructions.

Educational use only.
