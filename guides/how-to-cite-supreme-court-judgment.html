How to Cite a Supreme Court Judgment

Learn a practical format for citing Supreme Court of India judgments with case name, year, reporter, volume, and page details.

Basic structure

A Supreme Court judgment citation commonly identifies the case name, year, volume, reporter abbreviation, and first page or pinpoint reference.

Example

Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd.) v. Union of India, (2017) 10 SCC 1.

What to verify

Always check the reporter citation, page number, court requirements, and your institution or publication style guide before final submission.

Educational use only.

This guide explains citation structure. Always verify final citations against the relevant court, university, journal, or publisher requirement.
