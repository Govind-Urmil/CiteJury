How to Cite a Supreme Court Judgment

A Supreme Court of India judgment may be cited through a neutral citation, a reporter citation such as SCC or AIR, or a style required by your institution. The safest workflow is to identify which citation path is required before formatting.

Neutral citation anatomy

2023 INSC 154

  • 2023: year of judgment/order.
  • INSC: India Supreme Court neutral identifier.
  • 154: neutral sequence number.

Neutral citations are valuable because they identify a judgment independently of commercial reporters. They are still not a substitute for checking the official case title and judgment record.

Reporter citation anatomy

Reporter citations identify where the judgment appears in a report series. Common examples include SCC, AIR and SCR. Reporter details usually require a year, volume or court abbreviation, reporter abbreviation and opening page.

Which citation should you use?

  • Use neutral citation when your institution accepts or prefers official court-issued identifiers.
  • Use SCC/AIR when your institution, memorial, article, or court format specifically requires a reporter citation.
  • Use OSCOLA-style treatment only when your assignment or publisher asks for OSCOLA or an OSCOLA adaptation.

Required information in CiteJury for SCI neutral citation

  • Case title
  • Neutral citation year
  • Neutral sequence number

The field called “Neutral sequence number” is the number after INSC. If this is missing, CiteJury cannot produce the neutral citation.

Common mistakes

  • INSC 154 — year missing.
  • 2023 INSC — sequence number missing.
  • 2023 SCC 154 — mixes a reporter abbreviation into an incomplete reporter citation.
  • Using a news article title instead of the official case title.

Neutral citation versus law-report citation

A Supreme Court neutral citation identifies a decision independently of a commercial report series. For the supported SCI pattern, the year and neutral sequence are essential. A later SCC or AIR report citation is a different citation route and should be verified against that reporter.
