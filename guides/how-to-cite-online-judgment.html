How to Cite an Online Judgment

Learn how to cite judgments accessed online while preserving reliable case, court, year, reporter, and URL information.

Start with the source

Identify the authority accurately before formatting the citation. Record the title, author or parties, year, publication or reporter, volume, page, court, and URL where relevant.

Build the citation

Arrange the available components according to the citation style required by your court, university, journal, competition, or publisher.

Verify before submission

A citation generator can speed up formatting, but important citations should be checked against the original source and applicable style requirements.

Educational use only.
