How to Cite an Online Judgment

An online copy of a judgment is not automatically the best citation. Prefer official court information or a recognised reporter where available.

Trust-first note: CiteJury is a drafting and learning tool. Important citations should be verified against the original authority and the required style guide before submission.

Start with court data

Court name.

Case name.

Judgment date.

Neutral citation or official case number if available.

Reporter citation if you are using a reported version.

Online source details

Use URL and access date only where your citation style requires or where no better official citation is available.

Do not replace a recognised neutral/report citation with a bare URL unless your style guide permits it.

Verification

Compare the online copy with the official judgment or reporter if the citation is important.

Check paragraph numbers because uploaded PDFs and web versions may differ.

Examples and checks

Preferred approach

Case details first; URL only when needed.

Pinpoint habit

Use paragraph number when available rather than uncertain PDF page numbers.

Educational use only.

This guide explains citation structure and verification habits. It is not legal advice and does not replace a court, journal, university, publisher, or competition citation rule.
