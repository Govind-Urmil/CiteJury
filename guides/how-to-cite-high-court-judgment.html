How to Cite a High Court Judgment

A beginner-friendly guide to citing Indian High Court judgments using court, case name, year, reporter, and reference details.

Basic structure

High Court citations should clearly identify the parties, court, year, reporter or neutral citation where available, and relevant page or paragraph reference.

Court clarity

Mentioning the court or bench can help readers distinguish similar case names across jurisdictions.

Use CiteJury

Use the court/institution field in CiteJury to include High Court information in the generated citation.

Educational use only.

This guide explains citation structure. Always verify final citations against the relevant court, university, journal, or publisher requirement.
